How to play Guess & Pass

Every play freezes time for a 10-second decision window. Both sides commit secretly, then everything resolves at once. All players and the ball move at the same speed.

ATTACKING (you have the ball)

  • Commit the pass: TAP a teammate — he becomes the receiver (gold ring + pass line).
  • Send runners: SWIPE any teammate to a spot — they'll sprint there when play resumes.
  • Press LOCK IN early, or wait for the timer.
  • If the receiver collects the ball deep near the far goal, it becomes a shot.

DEFENDING (CPU has the ball)

  • Guess the receiver: TAP the opponent you think will get the pass (red ring). A correct read sends your defenders converging with a reach burst — touch the ball and you win possession!
  • Command your keeper: choose ◀ DIVE LEFT / STAY / DIVE RIGHT ▶. A correctly-placed dive saves the shot.

RULES

  • First to 3 goals wins · 24-play limit as tiebreaker.
  • Completed passes advance the attack; interceptions and saves hand possession to the other side.
  • Every match kicks off from the center spot.
