    PHASE 1 · VS CPU

    Guess & Pass

    Read the lane. Commit the pass. Break the pattern. Three seconds to decide — attacker and defender choose at the same time, hidden from each other.

    • Low-poly 3D players standing on a 2D top-view pitch.
    • First to 3 goals wins · hard cap 24 plays.
    • Resolution is the canonical geometric + guess-bonus algorithm (§4).
    HOW TO PLAY

    One window, two hidden choices

    • Attacking: swipe from the carrier toward a teammate to pick the pass — release on a teammate to commit early. Drag a teammate to move your runner. Or tap a teammate, then LOCK IN.
    • Defending: drag a defender outward to commit its guess direction — it breaks that way when the ball is played.
    • Everything is auto-filled at the end of the 3-second window: the attacker gets the safest receiver, defenders keep their current facing.
    • Keyboard: Enter / Space lock in · Esc pause · M mute · R restart match.
    • A pass that lands within shooting range (30 units) becomes a shot, resolved against the keeper.
