TUTORIAL

How to play

Every play freezes time for a 10-second window. Both sides commit secretly, then everything resolves at once. Players and the ball move at the same speed.

ATTACKING — you have the ball

  • TAP a teammate — he receives the pass (gold ring + line).
  • SWIPE any teammate to a spot — he sprints there when play resumes.
  • Receiver collecting the ball deep near goal = shot.

DEFENDING — CPU has the ball

  • TAP the opponent you think will receive (red ring).
  • SWIPE your defenders toward danger zones.
  • A correct read converges your defenders onto the receiver — touch the ball and it's yours!
  • Pick your keeper's dive for shots: ◀ / STAY / ▶.

RULES

  • First to 3 goals · 24-play limit tiebreaker.
  • Every match kicks off from the center spot.
